# banc_nt_ground_truth.zip

## Purpose

`banc_nt_ground_truth.zip` is the **train / test split** used to fit
and benchmark BANC's per-synapse neurotransmitter classifier. Each row
of either feather is one labelled presynaptic terminal — the position
of the synapse, the parent neuron's v888 `pt_root_id`, the cell type
the neuron belongs to, and the literature-verified neurotransmitter.
The split is used by the classifier-training pipeline in
`banc_888_neurotransmitter_prediction_v2` (paper Methods,
"Neurotransmitter prediction").

## Provenance

Curated by the BANC team from a literature review of cell-type
neurotransmitter assignments across FAFB, MANC, Hemibrain and BANC;
each neuron of a verified cell type contributes all of its predicted
presynapses as labelled training examples. The split was generated by
the BANC NT pipeline (Drugowitsch lab) on 2025-05-27 and frozen as
`v8_1`. The corresponding classifier output for all unlabelled BANC
presynapses is in
`banc_888_neurotransmitter_prediction_v2.parquet`.

## Files

| filename | rows | role |
|---|---:|---|
| `train_gt_synapses_250527_v8_1.feather` | 250 527 | full training pool; model fit on a stratified 16 448-synapse subset |
| `test_gt_synapses_250527_v8_1.feather` | 4 124 | held-out test set; classifier evaluation in Extended Data Fig. 3b |

## Schema

Both feathers share the same schema. (Column inventory will be
finalised when the deposit ZIP is built; the canonical fields are:
synapse `id`, `pt_position` (BANC voxel space), `pt_root_id` (v888),
`cell_type`, `nt_verified`, `dataset_source`, and the per-synapse
confidence assigned during literature curation.)

## Related files

- `banc_888_neurotransmitter_prediction_v2.parquet` — per-synapse
  output of the classifier fit on this ground truth. Contains the
  per-NT probabilities and the argmax prediction for every BANC
  presynapse at size threshold ≥ 5.
- `drosophila_neurotransmitters_archive.zip` — the literature review
  database that contributed cell-type-level NT calls. The ground-truth
  split here applies those cell-type calls to every presynapse owned by
  a verified neuron.
- `banc_888_synapses_v2_enriched.parquet` — the per-synapse table the
  classifier ran over; the GT split is a subset of these synapses with
  the `nt_verified` label attached.
- Extended Data Fig. 3b in the paper — confusion matrix evaluated on
  the held-out test set.

## Notes

- **The training / test split is fixed** — the deposit pins the
  `v8_1` (2025-05-27) version that the paper uses. Future iterations of
  the curation will land on GCS under a new dated suffix; check
  `gs://leelab_fly_cns/files/banc_nt_ground_truth/` for newer versions.
- **Reproducibility.** Pair this with `banc_888_neurotransmitter_prediction_v2.parquet`
  to retrain the classifier exactly as in the paper. The fitting code
  is in `banc_project_archive.zip`.
